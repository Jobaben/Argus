Living-data choreography

The one thing a live dashboard can do that a static report cannot is show change as change. A row that teleports to its new position shows nothing: you are left comparing the board against your memory of it a frame ago. Every reorder glides (FLIP), every arrival grows in, and every ambient indicator takes its phase from one shared clock — six things blinking on unrelated rhythms reads as clutter, in phase it reads as one system.
