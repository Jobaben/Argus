Motion lifecycle

Every surface that enters knows how to leave. The exit is the time-reverse of the entrance at 0.7× the duration, easing in rather than out. A surface that glides in and blinks out is the loudest “unfinished” tell an interface has — and dismissal is the most frequent thing anybody does.
